About Typing Mario

Typing Mario games are a fun way to improve your typing speed and accuracy. Players type words that appear on the screen to make Mario jump, run, and avoid obstacles. These games combine learning and fun, perfect for kids and adults alike!

How to Play

1. Start the game and watch the words appear on the screen.
2. Type each word correctly to make Mario move.
3. Avoid obstacles and complete levels as fast as possible.
4. Challenge yourself to improve your typing speed!
